Project Overview

Water logging and urban flooding are critical challenges in many cities worldwide, leading to severe infrastructure damage, traffic disruptions, and safety hazards. This project focuses on the development of an IoT-based water logging detection system that continuously monitors real-time water levels, rainfall intensity, and environmental conditions to provide early warnings and improve flood management strategies.

The system integrates ESP32 microcontrollers, water level sensors, rainfall sensors, and a cloud-based data visualization platform to enable smart monitoring and proactive response mechanisms. The data collected is uploaded to ThingSpeak and Firebase, ensuring real-time accessibility via a mobile application.

Technical Implementation

System Architecture

  • Sensor Data Acquisition: Water level, rainfall, temperature, and humidity data collection
  • Wireless Transmission (ESP32): Wi-Fi data transmission to cloud platforms
  • Cloud Data Processing & Storage: Data analysis using ThingSpeak and Firebase
  • Data Visualization & Alerts: Web dashboard and mobile app with automated alerts

Hardware Components

ESP32 Wi-Fi Microcontroller Water Level Sensors (x4) Rainfall Sensor (HW-028) DHT11 Sensor LED Indicators 3.7V LiPo Battery

Applications

Smart City Flood Management

Early detection of water logging and urban flooding. Helps municipal authorities plan drainage and water flow management.

Disaster Risk Reduction

Enables real-time flood prediction to alert disaster management teams. Supports evacuation planning in heavily flooded areas.

Transportation Safety

Assists traffic management by identifying flood-prone roads. Improves urban mobility planning during monsoon seasons.
